₱500 Office Exchange Gift Ideas (Philippines)

Twenty practical gifts close to ₱500, grouped by your officemate's hobby: coffee, pickleball, tennis and running, music, gadgets and the desk.

Updated Oct 13, 202612 min read20 picksPrices checked Oct 14, 2026

Heads up: links to Lazada on this page are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. This guide was researched with the help of AI tools. Here's how we pick.

It's monito-monita season again: names get drawn at the office, the budget is set, and you have a few weeks to find something your officemate will actually like.

₱500 is a realistic number. A 2018 YouGov survey found Filipino office workers were willing to spend about ₱500 on average for a Secret Santa gift, and that practical gifts were the most preferred. So every pick here is a useful gift close to that budget, grouped by hobby: find out what they're into, then jump to that section.

How we built this list

Close to ₱500, matched to their hobby

Gifting and career guides agree that the best office gifts match the person: find out what they enjoy outside work, then choose something practical within the group's budget. So we grouped this list by hobby and kept every pick close to ₱500, so nothing looks like an afterthought.

What we did

  1. Started from what officemates are into right now: coffee, pickleball, tennis and running, music and podcasts, gadgets, and long days at the desk.
  2. Shortlisted well-reviewed listings close to ₱500 for each hobby, then narrowed them to 20.
  3. Checked every product against Lazada's affiliate feed for price and stock on the day we published.
  4. Noted on each card when a pick is a little over budget, comes from a generic brand or a reseller, or plugs into the wall.
Shopping list based on: YouGov: Four in ten Filipino office workers will play Secret Santa · JobStreet PH: Last-minute gift ideas for your officemates and boss

How monito-monita works

Monito-monita (also called Kris Kringle) is the Filipino take on Secret Santa: names are drawn ahead of time, you keep your pick a secret, and the group agrees on a minimum and maximum price. Filipino groups often add fun theme categories to the exchange, so check if your office has one before you shop.

Sources: The Freeman (Philstar), GMA News

What to skip

Very personal gifts like perfume, cologne or pajamas, and gag gifts that could embarrass someone in front of the team. If you regift at all, it should be unopened, complete and in good condition, never a used item or an old corporate giveaway.

Sources: The Emily Post Institute, The Emily Post Institute, Rappler

For the coffee lover

Coffee lovers use their gear every day, so a good cup or grinder gets far more use than a novelty that ends up in a drawer. Most are ceramic, and each has a bit of personality, from a cat-face mug to a set that keeps coffee warm through long meetings.

  1. Not sure how they take their coffee? A cup, mug or warming set suits everyone from 3-in-1 fans to latte drinkers; save the grinder for someone who buys whole beans.
  2. With generic-brand ceramics, check the listing for a food-safe or lead-free note; the US FDA warns that some glazes can contain lead.
  3. Wrap cups and mugs in a kitchen towel before the kraft paper, and if you have budget left, add a small pack of kapeng barako or drip bags.

Halloween decor ideas: cute, not creepy, for small homes

Seven easy pieces for a cozy, cute-not-creepy Halloween at home: glowing ghost lights, orange and purple string lights, flying bats, a balloon garland and a ceramic pumpkin for the candy.

Updated Oct 14, 20268 min read7 picksPrices checked Oct 17, 2026

Heads up: links to Lazada on this page are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. This guide was researched with the help of AI tools. Here's how we pick.

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, 31 October 2026, and it's the perfect excuse to make home feel a little magical: a glowing ghost by the window, bats flying across the wall and a bowl of candy ready for the kids down the hall. You don't need a big house or a big budget to pull it off.

We kept this list cute, not creepy, and sized for Filipino condos and small homes. Most pieces hang, stick to a wall or sit on a shelf, and most pack away small for next year. The picks are ranked, so if you only buy one or two, start at the top.

How we built this list

Cute, not creepy, and easy to pack away

We chose friendly ghosts, smiling pumpkins, bats and an orange-and-purple glow over gory or jump-scare props, so the look works in homes with little kids. Every piece hangs, sticks to a wall or sits on a table, keeping the floor free in a small sala, and we added one ceramic pumpkin you'll keep using long after October.

Style it

Four tips for a cozy Halloween corner

Glow in orange and purple

Orange and purple are the most popular Halloween light colours, per Christmas Central's 2026 trend roundup. Pair them with one warm white string so the room feels cozy, not dim.

Source: Christmas Central: Trending Halloween decorations 2026

Layer at eye level

Hang ghost lights and bats between eye level and the ceiling, near a window or shelf, so the look reads from the sofa and the floor stays clear. Odd-numbered groups of three or five look fuller.

Common-sense advice

Pack it away for next year

Coil string lights around a piece of cardboard, keep bats and stickers flat in a folder, and take the batteries out of battery lights before storing so they don't leak.

Common-sense advice

Light safety first

For anything that plugs in, look for a PS mark or ICC sticker on the plug or box. Check cords for cracks or bare wire before use, and switch all lights off at bedtime or when you go out.

Source: DTI Bureau of Philippine Standards; US CPSC

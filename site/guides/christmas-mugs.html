Cute mugs for your own coffee corner

These are the mugs you keep for yourself: playful Christmas designs for your morning coffee, hot choco or milk. Set one out on a condo coffee corner or the breakfast table and the whole spot feels festive for the season.

  1. Check the capacity in the listing; a bigger mug suits a full cup of hot choco, a smaller one suits a quick coffee.
  2. Wash lids and figurine toppers by hand, even if the mug body is labeled dishwasher safe.
  3. Display your mugs on an open shelf or tray between uses so they double as Christmas decor.

Good to know

Four tips for buying and gifting mugs

Shop early: Christmas starts in September here

Pinoy Christmas kicks off with the -ber months, so holiday mugs get snapped up long before December. Ordering in October or November means you're not scrambling when party invites and exchange-gift draws start coming in.

Source: Malay Mail: Christmas comes early in the Philippines as -ber months kick off (9 Sep 2026)

Time your cart for 11.11 and payday sales

11.11 is one of the biggest online sale days of the year in the Philippines. Add your picks to cart ahead of time, then check out on sale day or around the payday sales at the end of October and mid-November, so the mugs arrive before your Christmas parties.

Source: ProductNation PH: 11.11 sale Philippines

Read the care details before you buy

Check the listing for capacity in ml and whether the mug is microwave or dishwasher safe. Lids, spoons and figurine toppers are best washed by hand, even if the mug itself can take the dishwasher.

Common-sense advice

Check ceramic mugs as soon as they arrive

Ceramic travels best well-padded. Open the parcel soon after delivery and check each mug, lid and topper for chips or cracks, so you can raise any issue with the seller while there's still time before gift-giving.

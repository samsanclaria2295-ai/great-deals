Rattan and woven finds for a boho condo

Six natural-fibre pieces, many from Filipino makers, that bring the relaxed boho look into a small condo: a rattan sunburst mirror, woven baskets, kaing planters, a seagrass tray, an acacia bowl and rattan coasters.

Updated Oct 12, 20267 min read6 picksPrices checked Oct 29, 2026

Heads up: links to Lazada on this page are affiliate links, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. This guide was researched with the help of AI tools. Here's how we pick.

Boho is the most forgiving style for a small home. It welcomes plants, texture and a little personality, and it ages well because natural fibres only look better with use.

Our country is also one of the best places in the world to shop it: rattan, abaca, buri and bamboo are woven here every day. So every piece below is a real natural material, and we favoured Filipino makers wherever we found them.

How we built this list

Curated, not cluttered

Homes & Gardens, House Beautiful and Livingetc agree that boho is relaxed and personality-led, built on natural fibres (rattan, wicker, jute, raffia, wood), plants and an earthy base with bolder accents, and that it should feel curated rather than cluttered. We applied that to a small Filipino condo: one woven statement on the wall, hard-working storage, then smaller repeated textures, using only real natural materials and favouring Filipino makers wherever the affiliate feed allowed.

What we did

  1. Read three design guides on boho style and noted what they agree on: natural fibres, plants, an earthy base and a few bold accents.
  2. Planned a small-condo version: one statement piece on the wall, storage that works, then smaller repeated textures.
  3. Compared 2 to 4 Lazada listings per slot, keeping only real natural materials (no plastic "rattan").
  4. Favoured named Filipino makers and checked every product photo against its listing.
Shopping list based on: Homes & Gardens: What is Bohemian style in interior design? · House Beautiful: 20 Boho Room Decor Ideas That Bring Good Vibes Into Your Home · Livingetc: Boho living rooms, the rules interior designers use to make spaces feel full of personality

Style it

Three rules for boho in a small space

Start with one statement

Start with one statement piece, like a rattan mirror on the main wall, then add texture in smaller doses. Livingetc's boho guide stresses curated over cluttered.

Source: Livingetc

Neutral base, bold accents

Keep walls and big furniture in earthy neutrals and let a banig, a cushion or a plant carry the bold colour, as Homes & Gardens suggests for boho palettes.

Source: Homes & Gardens

Give woven pieces air

Humidity care: give woven pieces air. Keep them off damp floors and away from aircon drips, wipe with a dry or barely damp cloth (Bria Homes says never rinse rattan), dry in shade, and never store abaca or raffia sealed in plastic, where mould can form.

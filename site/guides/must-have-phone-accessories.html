Creative doesn't mean careless, though. Chargers and power banks come only from well-known brands with model numbers, and we skipped look-alike stores and listings whose photos didn't match.

Creative phone holders and stands

These holders do more than prop up your phone: they fold flat, clip onto a bag, snap on with magnets or pop open into a tall tripod. Pick one for how you really use your phone, from video calls at the condo desk to barkada group shots.

  1. Magnetic stands snap straight onto iPhone 12 and newer (not the 16e). Android phones need a magnetic case or the metal ring some stands include.
  2. Using a big phone in a thick case? Test the stand on the table first before trusting it with an important shot.
  3. For group photos, set the tripod on flat ground, open the legs fully and use your camera timer or a Bluetooth remote.

Creative phone cases

Creative cases add a trick on top of protection: a built-in stand, a breathable mesh back, a bold print or a little mirror for touch-ups. They're affordable enough to switch with your mood, but each one is cut for an exact phone model.

  1. Cases fit one exact model. Pick yours from the listing's variant list and double-check it (iPhone 16 vs 16 Pro, Galaxy A55 vs A56).
  2. A case with built-in magnets lets Android phones use MagSafe-style stands, grips, wallets and chargers.
  3. Wipe clear and light cases weekly with mild soap and water, and brush out mesh holes so dust does not build up.

Creative chargers and power

Chargers and power banks don't have to be plain white bricks. These picks from official Anker and UGREEN stores add a robot face, a built-in stand or a fold-flat design, and still cover the basics: fast, safe charging from brands you can trace.

  1. Buy chargers and power banks only from the brand's official store, and read the seller name letter by letter: fake 'ΑNΚER' and 'АNKЕR' stores use Greek or Cyrillic look-alike letters.
  2. Own an Anker power bank? Check its model number against Anker's June 2025 recall (A1257, A1647, A1652, A1681, A1689) and stop using it if it matches.
  3. Keep power banks out of hot cars and direct sun, and pack them in your hand-carry bag, not checked luggage, when you fly.

Other creative accessories

Small add-ons that unlock new tricks: zoom and macro shots, a camera-style grip with its own light, and tags that help you find your keys. They make easy gifts because most work with almost any phone.

  1. Clip-on lenses work best without a thick case; centre the lens over your main camera and use a tripod for zoom shots.
  2. Apple Find My tags only work with iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch. Android users should choose a tag that supports Google's Find Hub.
  3. Charge grips and lights before a shoot, and switch the light off afterwards so it does not drain in your bag.

Dachshund decor and ornaments

These are the pieces that turn a regular Christmas setup into a dachshund one: a candle holder, felt figurines, plush dogs and tree ornaments. Mix one or two with your usual decor, or go all in for a full wiener-ful theme.

  1. Group figurines and plush dogs in twos or threes on one shelf; a little 'pack' looks cuter than one dog on its own.
  2. Hang light acrylic ornaments anywhere on the tree, and keep plush or heavier pieces on sturdy branches or on a table.
  3. Store felt and plush pieces in a sealed box with a silica pack after the season so they stay dry and dust-free.

Good to know

Four tips for a dog-friendly Christmas

Ride the dachshund trend, your way

Christmasworld spotted dachshunds in every style for 2026: felt, wood, glass, straw and knits, often in scarves and woolly hats. Pick one look, like cozy felt or bright acrylic, and repeat it on the tree, a shelf and the door so the theme feels planned.

Source: Christmasworld: Christmas tree trends 2026

Never leave a candle burning alone

If you use a real tealight in a candle holder, keep it away from the tree, curtains and anything that can burn, and blow it out before you leave the room or go to sleep. An LED tealight gives the same cozy glow with no flame.

Source: NFPA: Winter Holiday Safety tip sheet

Hang small ornaments up high

Little kids have small airways, so a swallowed ornament piece can be a choking risk. Put the smallest and most breakable pieces on the upper branches and make sure each one is secured to its branch, since even high ornaments can get knocked down. Curious pets are another good reason to keep small pieces up high.

Source: Poison Control: Christmas ornament hazards

Let your pet say no to the costume

Try a Christmas hat or bib on your dog or cat for a few minutes first, with treats. Make sure it isn't tight, never leave your pet alone in it, and take it off right away if they paw at it, hide or seem stressed.

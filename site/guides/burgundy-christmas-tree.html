Burgundy velvet ornaments

Velvet and flocked ornaments have a soft, matte, fabric-like surface instead of a shiny one, so burgundy looks deep and plush rather than bright. They're the base of this look: hang them all over the tree, then let gold and cream pieces do the sparkling.

  1. Hang the biggest burgundy balls deep inside the branches first, then work outward; it makes the tree look fuller.
  2. Spread the color evenly by stepping back every few ornaments and filling any empty patch.
  3. Brush off dust gently with a soft dry brush when you pack them away, and store them in their box so the velvet doesn't get crushed.

Style it

Four tips for a burgundy and gold Christmas

Stick to a short color list

Pick burgundy as your main color, add cream or white for contrast, and use gold as the shine. Christmasworld's Festive Opulence palette pairs cream, wine red and burgundy with gold and metallic finishes, so a tight palette like this looks planned, not random.

Source: Christmasworld: Christmas tree trends 2026 (press release)

Mix textures, not just colors

Velvet balls next to glossy gold beads and a chunky knit skirt give the tree depth even in one color family. Christmasworld also names knitted textiles and velvety flocked finishes among 2026's soft, cozy decor details.

Source: Christmasworld: Christmas tree trends 2026 (press release)

Keep candles away from fabric decor

Velvet ornaments, ribbons, tassels and knit skirts are all fabric. Keep lit candles away from decorations and anything that can burn, and turn off light strings before you go to bed or leave the house.

Source: NFPA: Winter Holiday Safety tip sheet

Hang ornaments securely, especially with kids around

Make sure every ornament is secured to a branch, since even high ones can still fall. With little kids at home, keep small or breakable pieces up high and out of reach.

Source: Poison Control: Christmas ornament hazards

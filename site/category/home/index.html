Our standards

What we look for in home and decor

Pieces that work together

We start from the look or the problem, list the pieces that create it, and only then search for products, so a guide reads like a plan, not a pile.

Good materials, no plastic

Glass, wood, stone, ceramic, cotton and native fibres like rattan, abaca, buri and capiz. If it looks or feels like cheap plastic, it does not make the list.

Sellers with a track record

Named brands, local makers and established stores with steady recent buyers, at prices that make sense for the item.

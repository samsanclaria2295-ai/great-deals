Our standards

How we'll pick beauty finds

Official and authorized sellers

We'll only recommend beauty products sold by the brand's own store or an authorized seller, because that's the surest way to get the genuine item.

Claims checked against sources

We'll look at ingredient lists and claims and compare them with reputable sources, and say plainly when evidence is thin.

Made for our weather

We'll favour products that suit hot, humid days and a range of Filipino skin types, with notes on who each one is for.

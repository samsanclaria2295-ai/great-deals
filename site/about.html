About Great Finds

How we pick what we recommend

Great Finds is a small, independent project run by one person in the Philippines. The goal is simple: help Filipino shoppers find products that are actually worth their money, and be honest about the ones that aren't.

What we cover

Tech and gadgets, home and decor, beauty and skin care, and seasonal finds like Christmas decor and sale-season picks. The topics come from research into what people are shopping for, and each guide focuses on products available to buyers in the Philippines.

The four checks

1. Real listings first

We work from Lazada's affiliate product data: current price, stock and recent purchases. We never copy or scrape Lazada's website.

2. Risky listings out

We drop listings with odd prices, brand names stuffed into unrelated products, look-alike store names or recalled model numbers, even when they sell well.

3. Research, with sources

We read manufacturer pages, independent reviews and safety guidance, and cross-check our notes with AI research assistants (Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT). Claims link back to their source.

4. Straight talk

Every pick gets pros and cons, who it suits and what to check before buying. Where the evidence is thin, we say so on the page.

What we won't do

  • Say we tested a product when we haven't. Unless a guide says otherwise, we haven't handled the products ourselves.
  • Invent reviews, ratings or testimonials.
  • Hide the downsides of a product to make it sound better.
  • Show a product photo that's been edited. Product photos come straight from each Lazada listing.

How we use AI

AI helps us research and write faster, but it doesn't replace checking. We use AI research assistants to gather and compare sources, and every claim about a product links to a source you can read yourself. Room and still-life pictures marked "AI sample render" are generated with Google's Gemini image model from text descriptions. They show the overall look with items similar to our picks; they are not photos of the actual products.

How Great Finds earns

Some links on Great Finds are affiliate links. We're part of Lazada's affiliate program, so if you buy through one of our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Great Finds is an independent site, not operated by Lazada, and no brand has paid to appear in our guides.

Prices and updates

Prices are shown as "from around ₱X" with the date we checked them, because online prices and options change often. Always check the final price, the option you're choosing and the seller details on Lazada before you buy.

Spotted something off?

If a detail is wrong or out of date, or a product disappoints you, we want to know. Email samsanclaria2295@gmail.com and we'll look into it and fix the guide.
